AI-assisted development

PiiGUI has been developed with assistance from several AI systems,
including:

- DeepSeek models
- locally hosted open-weight models
- OpenAI models
- Google Gemini models
- Anthropic Claude models

AI-generated suggestions were reviewed, modified, tested and
integrated by the project author.
